HOW I WORK

My Outreach Process

A structured, repeatable approach refined over 10 years — turning cold prospects into activated, revenue-generating partners.

01

Affiliate Research & Qualification

Finding relevant affiliates based on niche, audience fit, engagement, content quality, and promotional potential. Every prospect is vetted before outreach to ensure genuine fit with the offer.

02

Outreach & Follow-Up

Personalized outreach with structured follow-ups to build relationships and increase response rates. No generic blasts — every message speaks to the prospect's audience and goals.

03

Media Kit & Promotional Materials

Organizing product information, key selling points, visuals, testimonials, banners, email copy, social captions, content ideas, FAQs, and other resources affiliates can use to promote effectively.

04

Affiliate Signup & Tracking

Reviewing the signup process and making sure affiliates can easily access their affiliate links, offers, and tracking information — removing friction from day one.

05

Affiliate Guidelines

Ensuring partners understand approved promotional methods, brand messaging, disclosure requirements, and program rules so every promotion stays compliant and on-brand.

06

Onboarding & Activation

Helping new affiliates understand the offer, access their promotional resources, and take the necessary steps toward their first promotion. The goal: active partners, not just signups.

07

Ongoing Follow-Up

Maintaining communication with recruited affiliates, supporting active partners, and re-engaging promising affiliates who have not yet started promoting. Relationships don't end at signup.
